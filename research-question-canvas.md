# Interface Critique & Research Question Canvas

## Selected Workflow
College certificate submission process.

## Problem Statement
Students may struggle to understand document requirements, upload certificates correctly, and track their submission status.

## Primary Research Question
How do college students navigate the certificate submission process, and what difficulties do they experience?

## Assumptions to Validate
1. Students may be unsure about required documents and accepted file formats.
2. Students may need clearer submission confirmation.
3. Students may find application statuses and next steps confusing.

## Heuristic Review
- Clarity: Are instructions easy to understand?
- Feedback: Does the system confirm actions?
- Error prevention: Are invalid uploads and missing fields prevented?
- Accessibility: Are text, contrast, and labels usable?
- Trust: Are deadlines and application statuses transparent?

## Research Method
Observe 3–5 students completing the workflow. Record their difficulties and validate the assumptions before proposing improvements.

## Evidence Required
- At least five actual screenshots or states.
- Annotations explaining usability problems.
- Research observations and design opportunities.
- Completed heuristic review.

## Deliverables
Figma critique board and one-page research question canvas with annotated evidence.
